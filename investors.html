Thesis & Market Opportunity
Sequoia Capital March 5, 2026 Thesis
Services: The New Software
AI has crossed the threshold from copilot to autopilot — enabling companies to sell outcomes rather than tools. The next trillion-dollar company will look like a services firm. Autopilots capture labor budgets that are an order of magnitude larger than software budgets.
Orbit's entire model is the execution layer of this thesis. Every acquisition is a bet that AI-native services businesses — not software platforms — are the generational opportunity.
Read → sequoiacap.com
Andreessen Horowitz November 2024 Thesis
Vertical SaaS: Now with AI Inside
AI launches a third wave of vertical SaaS — turning platforms into full-stack operators that 2–10× revenue per customer by automating sales, support, finance, and ops. Field services companies that own workflow data become the highest-value AI plays.
The Nexus AI stack is exactly this: AI layered into recurring field services businesses to multiply per-customer economics. Orbit is building what a16z describes as the defensible vertical AI opportunity.
Read → a16z.com
General Catalyst 2025 Market Context
The Future of Services
U.S. services industries generate $6T+ annually versus a $370B software market — yet remain largely untouched by AI. GC frames AI-enabled roll-ups in services as a market opportunity that dwarfs the entire SaaS wave.
Orbit operates at the intersection GC identifies: recurring services businesses where AI transforms delivery economics, not just workflow efficiency. The TAM is an order of magnitude larger than it appears.
Read → generalcatalyst.com
Sequoia Capital December 2024 Market Context
AI in 2025: Building Blocks Firmly in Place
By end of 2025, AI infrastructure will be locked in — shifting competitive advantage toward application-layer companies capturing labor budgets rather than software budgets. AI displaces labor, not software, attacking a profit pool an order of magnitude larger than cloud.
Orbit's timing is deliberate. Portfolio companies with Nexus integrated today will have 12–24 months of compounding data advantage over acquirers deploying in 2027.

McKinsey & Company 2025 M&A Strategy
Technology M&A: AI Enters Its Industrial Phase
Tech M&A has shifted from isolated AI experiments to systematic, portfolio-wide integration. Acquirers deploying AI across their entire portfolio are generating significantly higher value creation than traditional software roll-ups.
Nexus AI is Orbit's integration layer. McKinsey's data supports the thesis that systematic AI deployment across acquired businesses — not one-off automation — is where durable value is created post-close.

Andreessen Horowitz December 2024 Pricing & Revenue
AI Is Driving A Shift Towards Outcome-Based Pricing
AI forces companies away from per-seat models toward usage- and outcome-based pricing as AI agents deliver work rather than assist humans. The value capture layer in software is moving from access to outcomes.
This is the monetization model Orbit's Nexus stack enables. Portfolio companies that move to outcome-based pricing post-acquisition capture a fundamentally larger share of customer value.
