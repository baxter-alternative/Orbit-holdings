1. Executive Summary

Purpose

This playbook provides a standardized framework for evaluating and entering new vertical markets. It enables:

  • Comparable ROI analysis across markets and channels
  • Clear exit criteria at each investment stage
  • Disciplined capital allocation based on validated metrics
  • Repeatable process for market expansion

Core Principles

  1. Stage-gated investment — Increase spend only as metrics validate
  2. Channel attribution — Track ROI by channel to optimize allocation
  3. Referral-adjusted economics — Account for multiplier effects in partner-heavy markets
  4. Kill criteria — Know when to exit before overspending

2. Phase 1: Research

2.1 Secondary Research

Objective: Establish market fundamentals before investing in primary research.

Activity Data Sources Key Outputs
Market sizing U.S. Census CBP, IBISWorld, industry surveys TAM/SAM/SOM by segment
Segment analysis NAICS codes, industry reports ICP definition by firm type
Competitive mapping Vendor websites, G2/Capterra, pricing pages Feature/price matrix
Growth dynamics Industry associations, analyst reports Segment growth rates
Tech stack analysis Integration marketplaces, job postings Ecosystem entry points

Secondary Research Checklist

  • Total addressable market (TAM) quantified
  • Market segmented by firm type/size/geography
  • Growth rates by segment identified
  • 5+ competitors mapped with pricing
  • Key integrations/tech stack identified
  • Industry associations and events listed
  • Regulatory/compliance requirements noted

2.2 Primary Research

Objective: Validate secondary findings and uncover buying behavior.

Partner Interview Framework

Topic Area Sample Questions
Discovery behavior "How did you hear about [product]? Do you actively search for AR solutions?"
Peer influence "What associations/peer groups do you belong to? How do you learn about new tools?"
Pain points "Walk me through your invoicing process before [product]. What caused you to seek alternatives?"
Decision criteria "What 2-3 features drove your decision? How price-sensitive are you?"
Referral potential "How much do clients trust your software recommendations? Have you recommended [product]?"
Competitive intel "What other solutions did you evaluate? What do you hear about [competitor]?"

Primary Research Checklist

  • 5-10 partner/customer interviews completed
  • 3+ pain points validated
  • Buying behavior documented
  • Competitive perception captured
  • Referral potential assessed
  • Key quotes for messaging collected

3.2 Conference Playbook

Pre-Event (4-6 weeks out)

  • Pull target attendee list from registration/sponsorship package
  • Cross-reference against enriched prospect list
  • Schedule 10-15 meetings with high-fit prospects pre-event
  • Prepare case study one-pagers with quantified outcomes
  • Create vertical-specific messaging collateral
  • Brief booth staff on ICP, messaging, qualification criteria
  • Set up lead capture system and follow-up sequences

At-Event

  • Execute pre-scheduled meetings
  • Staff booth with qualified personnel
  • Lead with pain validation in conversations
  • Demo key differentiating features
  • Capture: firm size, # clients, current stack, pain level (1-5), follow-up permission
  • Host dinner/event for high-value prospects (Tier 1 only)

Booth Conversation Framework

1. QUALIFY
   "Do you manage AR for your clients, or just your own firm?"
   "How many clients are you currently serving?"

2. PAIN PROBE
   "What's your biggest headache with [current solution]?"
   "How much time does your team spend on collections?"

3. DIFFERENTIATE
   "What if you could [key differentiator]?"
   "Our customers typically see [quantified outcome]..."

4. PROOF
   "Firms like [reference] save [X hours/dollars]..."

5. NEXT STEP
   "Can I show you a 15-minute demo next week?"
   "Who else should be on that call?"

Post-Event (within 72 hours)

  • Send personalized follow-up referencing specific pain discussed
  • Share relevant case study based on firm profile
  • Book demos within 2 weeks
  • Add all leads to nurture sequence
  • Calculate preliminary event ROI
  • Debrief with team on learnings

5.2 Fully-Loaded Cost Calculation

Fully-Loaded Channel Cost =
    Direct Spend
    + (Allocated Headcount × Hourly Rate × Hours)
    + Overhead %

Example - Conference:
├── Sponsorship:                    $15,000
├── Booth/collateral:                $3,000
├── Travel (2 people × 3 days):      $4,000
├── SDR time (40 hrs × $50):         $2,000
├── AE time (20 hrs × $75):          $1,500
└── FULLY-LOADED COST:              $25,500

5.4 LTV Calculations

Standard LTV

LTV = (Monthly Revenue × Gross Margin %) × Avg Customer Lifespan (months)

Example:
├── Monthly Revenue:     $500
├── Gross Margin:        70%
├── Avg Lifespan:        36 months
└── LTV:                 $500 × 0.70 × 36 = $12,600

Referral-Adjusted LTV

For markets with strong referral dynamics (e.g., accounting, MSP):

Referral-Adjusted LTV =
    Direct LTV
    + (Referral Rate × Referrals per Customer × Referred Customer LTV)

Example:
├── Direct LTV:                    $12,600
├── Referral Rate:                 40% of customers refer
├── Avg Referrals per Customer:    5 clients
├── Referred Client LTV:           $6,000
├── Referral Value:                0.40 × 5 × $6,000 = $12,000
└── REFERRAL-ADJUSTED LTV:         $12,600 + $12,000 = $24,600

Ratio Interpretation Action
< 1:1 Losing money Exit or major pivot
1:1 - 2:1 Marginal Optimize or reduce spend

4:1+ Strong Scale aggressively

6. Stage-Gate Investment Model

6.1 Stage Overview

Stage Investment Duration Objective
Stage 1: Research $5-15K 4-6 weeks Validate market opportunity
Stage 2: Test $25-50K 8-12 weeks Prove initial traction
Stage 3: Validate $75-150K 2 quarters Prove repeatable economics
Stage 4: Scale $250K+/yr Ongoing Maximize market capture

6.2 Stage 1: Research

Activities

  • Secondary research (market sizing, competitive mapping)
  • 5-10 partner/customer interviews
  • Initial list build
  • ICP definition

Exit Criteria (Must Meet ALL)

Criterion Target
TAM > $50M addressable revenue
Pain points 3+ validated pain points our product solves
Competitive whitespace Not saturated; clear positioning opportunity
Prospect availability Path to 100+ qualified prospects
Reference customer At least 1 existing customer willing to be reference

Decision

  • PROCEED to Stage 2 if all criteria met
  • STOP or DEFER if criteria not met

6.3 Stage 2: Test

Activities

  • 1 conference
  • Targeted outbound (500 contacts)
  • 1 partnership exploration
  • Vertical landing page/content

Exit Criteria (Must Meet 3 of 4)

Criterion Target
Leads 50+ generated
SQLs 10+ created
Opportunities 5+ opened
Pipeline:Spend > 3:1 ratio

Decision

  • PROCEED to Stage 3 if 3+ criteria met
  • STOP, PIVOT, or EXTEND test if < 3 criteria met

6.4 Stage 3: Validate

Activities

  • 2-3 conferences
  • Scaled outbound (2,000+ contacts)
  • Partnership launch
  • Dedicated SDR allocation
  • Vertical messaging and content

Exit Criteria

See Section 7: Stage 3 Validation Criteria (Detailed)

Decision

  • SCALE — All primary + 4+ secondary criteria met
  • MAINTAIN — All primary, < 4 secondary met
  • OPTIMIZE — 4-5 primary met, clear fix path
  • REDUCE — 2-3 primary met, not strategic
  • EXIT — < 2 primary met or fundamental issues

6.5 Stage 4: Scale

Activities

  • Full conference calendar
  • Dedicated SDR(s)
  • Partner program
  • Vertical product investment
  • Content engine

Ongoing Thresholds (Quarterly Review)

Criterion Target
LTV:CAC > 3:1
CAC trend Stable or improving
Pipeline:Spend > 5:1
Market share Growing (logo count, revenue %)

If thresholds missed 2 consecutive quarters → Trigger review

6.6 Exit Decision Matrix

Signal LTV:CAC > 3:1 LTV:CAC 2-3:1 LTV:CAC < 2:1
Pipeline:Spend > 5:1 SCALE OPTIMIZE DIAGNOSE
Pipeline:Spend 3-5:1 MAINTAIN OPTIMIZE REDUCE
Pipeline:Spend < 3:1 DIAGNOSE REDUCE EXIT

Action Definitions

Action Definition
SCALE Increase investment, add headcount, expand channels
MAINTAIN Continue current investment, monitor for improvement
OPTIMIZE Hold spend flat, improve conversion/efficiency
DIAGNOSE Pause new spend, analyze root cause, test hypotheses
REDUCE Cut to maintenance level (minimal presence)

6.7 Kill Criteria (Immediate Stop)

Trigger immediate market exit review if ANY occur:

Criterion Threshold
Zero closed-won 0 customers after Stage 2 completion
Conversion collapse SQL→Opp rate < 10% for 8+ weeks
Product-market gap 3+ lost deals citing missing core feature
Competitive loss rate > 70% losses to single competitor

Churn signal > 30% of early customers churn in Year 1

7.7 Decision Definitions

SCALE (All must be true)

  • All 6 primary criteria met
  • 4+ secondary criteria met
  • No red early warning indicators in final month
  • At least 2 channels performing above target
  • Clear path to $500K+ ARR in 12 months

Actions: 2-3× budget, dedicated headcount, full conference calendar, product investment

MAINTAIN (All must be true)

  • All 6 primary criteria met
  • < 4 secondary criteria met
  • At least 1 channel performing well
  • No systemic issues (product gaps, competitive losses)

Actions: Continue current investment, monitor for improvement

OPTIMIZE (Typical profile)

  • 4-5 primary criteria met
  • 1-2 primary criteria narrowly missed
  • Clear hypothesis for improvement
  • Unit economics fixable with tactical changes

Actions: Hold spend flat, improve conversion/efficiency

REDUCE (Typical profile)

  • Only 2-3 primary criteria met
  • LTV:CAC marginal (1.5-2:1)
  • No channel performing well
  • Market not strategic priority

Actions: Cut to maintenance level (minimal presence)

EXIT (Any of these)

  • < 2 primary criteria met
  • LTV:CAC < 1.5:1
  • Zero closed-won customers
  • Fundamental product-market gap
  • > 70% competitive loss rate to single player

Actions: Wind down activities, reassign resources, document learnings

7.8 Review Cadence

Weekly Checkpoint (15 min)

  • Lead/SQL count vs. pace
  • Pipeline additions
  • Early warning indicator check
  • Blocker identification

Monthly Business Review (60 min)

  • Funnel metrics vs. target (10 min)
  • Channel performance breakdown (15 min)
  • Deal review: wins, losses, stalls (15 min)
  • Customer feedback/signals (10 min)
  • Budget tracking (5 min)
  • Decisions/adjustments (5 min)

Mid-Stage Review — Month 3 (90 min)

  • Performance vs. plan (20 min)
  • Channel deep-dive (20 min)
  • Win/loss analysis (15 min)
  • Competitive update (10 min)
  • Customer health (10 min)
  • Budget reallocation (10 min)
  • Go/no-go decision (5 min)

Stage Exit Review — Month 6 (2 hours)

  • Stage 3 results summary (15 min)
  • Unit economics deep-dive (20 min)
  • Channel performance & learnings (20 min)
  • Sales motion & win/loss (20 min)
  • Customer success & referrals (15 min)
  • Competitive & market update (10 min)
  • Recommendation presentation (10 min)
  • Discussion & decision (10 min)
